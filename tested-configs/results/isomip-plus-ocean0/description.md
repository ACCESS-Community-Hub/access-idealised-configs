    - **Vertical coordinate:** `SIGMA_SHELF_ZSTAR`
    - **Topography:** `TOPO_CONFIG = ISOMIP`
    - **Surface forcing:** `WIND_CONFIG = zero`, `BUOY_CONFIG = NONE`
    - **Ice shelf:** yes
    - **Run length:** 90 days
