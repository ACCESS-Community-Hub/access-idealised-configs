!!! abstract "Description (as on the Overview)"
    "easily-relocatable single-column ACCESS-OM3-MOM6 configurations based on the single-column MOM6 configurations in the ocean_only/single_column directory of the MOM6-examples repository", with WOMBAT biogeochemistry ([README](https://github.com/ACCESS-Community-Hub/om3-mom6-single-column#readme)). Maintained by `dougiesquire` and `anton-seaice`.

    Tested set-up, read from this run's `MOM_parameter_doc.all`:

    - **Grid:** 2 x 2 points, 75 layers/levels (`GRID_CONFIG = cartesian`)
    - **Vertical coordinate:** `ZSTAR`
    - **Topography:** `TOPO_CONFIG = flat`
